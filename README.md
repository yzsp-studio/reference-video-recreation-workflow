把模糊的「照着重做」变成可控、可追溯的参考视频复刻流程。用户提供原片与明确改动（可附品牌或替换素材）后，技能拆解镜头结构与证据，锁定可核对的变更脚本，确认文字与元素决定，继承原片的运动、节奏、摄影与声音关系，只生成已批准范围。验收按构图、动作、时序、文字与音画要求逐项核对，结果会标明已实现、近似、未验证与失败项，并优先用现有素材做最小修复，而不是盲目重复消耗。交付复刻成片、版本记录与真实可编辑范围说明，适合宣传片、产品发布片与动态图形复刻。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 上传原片 + 改动说明 → 输出可核对的变更脚本与复刻成片
- 拆解镜头结构、信息顺序、视觉层级、动作触发与停顿、转场连接、声音与节奏
- 继承原片运动、节奏、摄影与声音关系，未特别说明的部分默认沿用原片
- 逐项音画验收并标明已实现 / 近似 / 未验证 / 失败，不用虚构相似度分数代替证据
- 优先用现有素材做最小修复，并说明相邻镜头影响，不自行批准新增生成消耗
- 交付成片 + 版本记录 + 真实可编辑范围说明，能力缺口如实说明而不静默降标

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a vague "remake this" into a controlled, traceable recreation workflow. Given the source video plus explicit changes and any brand or replacement assets, the skill breaks down shot structure and source evidence, locks a reviewable change script, confirms exact text and element decisions, inherits the original motion, rhythm, camera and sound relationships, and generates only the approved scope. Validation checks composition, action, timing, typography and audiovisual requirements item by item, reporting what is achieved, approximate, unverified or failed, and prefers minimal repair with existing assets over blind repeated spending. Delivery includes the recreated video, version history and an honest statement of what remains editable — best for promos, launch films and motion-graphics recreations.

### ✨ What it does

- Source video plus change notes in → a reviewable change script and the recreated cut out
- Breaks down shot structure, information order, visual hierarchy, action triggers and pauses, transition joins, sound and pacing
- Inherits motion, rhythm, camera and sound relationships from the source; anything unspecified follows the original by default
- Validates audiovisuals item by item and labels results achieved / approximate / unverified / failed instead of inventing similarity scores
- Prefers minimal repair with existing assets, states the impact on adjacent shots, and never self-approves new spend
- Delivers the video, version history and a truthful statement of editable range, with capability gaps reported rather than silently downgraded
